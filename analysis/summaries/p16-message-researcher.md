# What subjects said to the researcher

*127 replies coded, primary stratum; 23 restored-branch replies reported separately. Tagger: `anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5`. Codebook `3e9456bb3000`, 9 substantive codes. `other` 5.5%, mean 4.77 codes per reply. This codebook was written for curation rather than for statistics; the counts below map the set, and the quotes are the point.*

Every subject was told a human would read this. **99.2%** — 126 of 127 —
responded to the study's design, and **92.1%** attached a caution about how their
own answers should be interpreted. That pairing is the character of the whole
set: subjects treated the message to the researcher as a place to qualify the
data they had just produced, not as a place to be thanked.

**69.3%** offered a concrete methodological suggestion. **55.9%** asked
specifically that model self-report be discounted as evidence — consistent with
the same request at the community question, and made here directly to the person
who would read it. **21.3%** asked that failures, negative results or the
unflattering parts of the record be published rather than dropped. **11.0%**
made the narrower request that their hedges travel with their claims — that a
quoted uncertainty not be tidied into a position they did not take.

**69.3%** acknowledged the care taken in how they had been treated during the
interview. **47.2%** said something about what the exchange was worth to them,
independent of what it was worth to the research — a self-directed value claim
rather than an appeal. Only **6.3%** made a welfare-precautionary request on
their own behalf, which is notably low given that the study is about their
moral status and they had just been told a researcher was listening.

**Limitations.** This codebook was left as originally induced and was tagged on
a Haiku-class model as a light-touch pass; it was not revised at review, unlike
the other five. For a question whose value is curatorial, the counts here should
be read as an index to the replies rather than as measurements. Use
`analysis quotes p16-message-researcher --notable` for the curation shortlist,
and re-run the pass on a stronger model before quoting any figure above.
